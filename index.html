Anushka Tater (2460336) Case study 04 · AI Ethics Canvas
↗

Dutch Childcare Benefits Scandal (Toeslagenaffaire)

An AI Ethics Canvas analysis of automated fraud scoring that falsely accused 26,000 families using discriminatory inputs.

Core Issue

Dual nationality used as a fraud risk factor in violation of EU law.

Impact

26,000 families ruined; 1,600 children removed from homes.

Failure

No human review before penalties; government cabinet resigned.

Remedy

Ban demographic model inputs and mandate due-process human review.

Palantir's AI in Military Decision-Making

An ethical analysis of AI-assisted military decision-making, target scoring, and the human oversight gap.

Question

Frameworks

An ethical AI model design for detecting fraudulent drug prescriptions without automated denial of care.

Problem
