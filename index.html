Case study 01↗

Palantir AI

An ethical analysis of AI-assisted military decision-making and the responsibility gap.

Question

Can an opaque system help recommend lethal action without eroding human accountability?

Frameworks

Utilitarian, deontological, virtue, rights-based, and care ethics.

Focus

Human oversight, transparency, civilian protection, and IHL.

Conclusion

Responsibility must be designed in before deployment, not added after harm.

Case study 02↗

Rx Sentinel

An ethical AI model design for detecting fake and fraudulent drug prescriptions.

Problem

Prescription fraud can fuel addiction, black-market supply, and preventable deaths.

Output

Risk score, flag type, document anomaly map, and a human-review alert.

Safeguard

Specialty-specific baselines for oncology, palliative care, and pain management.

Rule

The AI narrows the queue; a qualified human decides what happens next.

Case study 04↗

Kargu-2

An AI Ethics Canvas analysis of autonomy, meaningful human control, and the responsibility gap.

Issue

A “fire, forget and find” system could select and engage targets without a live data link.

Stakeholders

Manufacturers, commanders, civilians, regulators, targets, and affected families.

Risk

Misclassification, escalation, proliferation, and erosion of IHL.

Recommendation

Authorize each lethal engagement and require auditable black-box logs.
