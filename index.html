Responsible AI Tool Usage & Fact-Checking Protocol

In accordance with Responsible AI guidelines, Generative AI tools were used transparently for content creation, idea generation, design support, and ethical analysis support. Below is our tool disclosure and human verification protocol:

AI Tools Employed

Tools Used: [ChatGPT / Google Gemini / Claude / Midjourney] (Placeholder — update specifics)
Applications: Idea generation, drafting outlines, poster concept design, and preliminary policy queries.

Verification & Fact-Checking

Every AI-generated summary was cross-checked against primary sources (live privacy policies, UN reports, legal statutes). Prompt outputs were explicitly tested for hallucinations (e.g., false ad-targeting claims in Khan Academy audit).

Limitations & Bias Awareness

We actively monitored for pattern-matching bias, training cutoff limits, and commercial dark-pattern over-generalizations. AI was treated as a first-pass auditor, never a final authority.

Team Reflection Summary

"Building this portfolio reinforced that responsible data and AI practice is not merely about legal compliance, but about human dignity, agency, and accountability. As a team, we observed how easily opaque algorithms create responsibility vacuums—whether in defense targeting, automated fraud scoring, or price coordination. We learned that Generative AI can accelerate synthesis, but human critical evaluation remains non-negotiable to catch hallucinations and structural biases. True ethical design must be embedded before deployment, not added after harm."

— Aman (2460324) & Anushka Tater (2460336) · Team Reflection

Dutch Childcare Benefits Scandal (Toeslagenaffaire)

An AI Ethics Canvas analysis of automated fraud scoring that falsely accused 26,000 families using discriminatory inputs.

Core Issue

Dual nationality used as a fraud risk factor in violation of EU law.

Impact

26,000 families ruined; 1,600 children removed from homes.

Failure

No human review before penalties; government cabinet resigned.

Remedy

Ban demographic model inputs and mandate due-process human review.
